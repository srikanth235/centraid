# Home-app journey budget

The five journeys `docs-drive`, `agenda-week`, `notes-library`, `tasks-board` and `locker-gate` share one gateway process and one paired simulator profile. `run-home-apps-suite.mjs` pairs fresh during the Docs journey and the remaining four reuse that paired profile (`MAESTRO_REUSE_PAIRED_STATE=1`). The runner fails when aggregate wall time is **ten minutes or more**, measured from the first flow process start through the fifth verdict. Every journey still writes an independent verdict, including after an earlier failure.

**Where ten minutes comes from.** This is a FIRST-LAND ceiling, not an observed number plus headroom — nothing in this suite has run on a device yet, so there is no distribution to sit on top of. It is derived from the one measured neighbour: `run-photos-suite.mjs` holds five journeys over one app cover at eight minutes, and the harness prices a fresh pairing at roughly four of those minutes on the reviewed CI runner. These five open five different covers and three of them push a second route, so two extra minutes of navigation is the honest allowance. The moment the nightly lane produces three real runs, the ceiling should be re-derived from the observed p95 and TIGHTENED — a budget nothing has ever come close to is not a budget.

If two consecutive nightly runs exceed ten minutes, first combine adjacent Maestro chunks and remove duplicate arrival assertions. If that is insufficient, fold `locker-gate`'s post-restart chunk into the restart smoke that `native-v0-resilience` already pays for, preserving both claims. Do not add retries and do not weaken a structural assertion to buy time.
