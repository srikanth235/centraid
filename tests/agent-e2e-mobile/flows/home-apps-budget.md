# Home-app journey budget

The seven journeys `docs-drive`, `agenda-week`, `notes-library`, `tasks-board`, `people-roster`, `tally-derived` and `locker-gate` share one gateway process and one paired simulator profile. `run-home-apps-suite.mjs` pairs fresh during the Docs journey and the remaining six reuse that paired profile (`MAESTRO_REUSE_PAIRED_STATE=1`). The runner fails when aggregate wall time is **twelve minutes or more**, measured from the first flow process start through the seventh verdict. Every journey still writes an independent verdict, including after an earlier failure.

**Where ten minutes came from.** This is a FIRST-LAND ceiling, not an observed number plus headroom — nothing in this suite has run on a device yet, so there is no distribution to sit on top of. It was derived from the one measured neighbour: `run-photos-suite.mjs` holds five journeys over one app cover at eight minutes, and the harness prices a fresh pairing at roughly four of those minutes on the reviewed CI runner. The original five opened five different covers and three of them pushed a second route, so two extra minutes of navigation was the honest allowance — eight plus two.

**Where the eleventh minute comes from.** `people-roster` (#864) is the sixth journey. It does not pay the boot or the pairing again — those are already inside the ten — so its whole marginal cost is its own navigation: one more app cover opened, and one more push to a second route. The ten-minute derivation priced navigation at two minutes across eight units of it (five covers plus three second-route pushes), which is fifteen seconds a unit; People's two units are half a minute at that rate. Rounded up to a whole minute, because a ceiling built on a rate nobody has measured should round against itself, not in its own favour. The moment the nightly lane produces three real runs, the ceiling should be re-derived from the observed p95 and TIGHTENED — a budget nothing has ever come close to is not a budget, and this one now carries a minute of arithmetic on top of a minute of arithmetic.

**Where the twelfth minute comes from.** `tally-derived` (#873) is the seventh journey. Like People it pays neither the boot nor the pairing again, so its whole marginal cost is its own navigation: one more app cover opened, and one band tap to a second place within it. At the same fifteen-seconds-a-unit rate the ten-minute derivation used, its two units are half a minute — rounded up to a whole minute for the same reason People's was, because a ceiling built on an unmeasured rate should round against itself. This is now a minute of arithmetic on a minute of arithmetic on a minute of arithmetic, which is the strongest possible argument for re-deriving the whole ceiling from observed p95 the moment the nightly lane produces three real runs.

If two consecutive nightly runs exceed twelve minutes, first combine adjacent Maestro chunks and remove duplicate arrival assertions. If that is insufficient, fold `locker-gate`'s post-restart chunk into the restart smoke that `native-v0-resilience` already pays for, preserving both claims. Do not add retries and do not weaken a structural assertion to buy time.

## What #890 added, and why the ceiling did not move

[#890](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/890) W5 put the **capture trio** inside three of these journeys — a Notes quick-capture that survives a real process death, an Agenda event composed and found in the widened Schedule read, and a Tasks quick-add that lands in the Inbox the capture bar itself names — and gave `docs-drive` a pin-answer plus an offline open on Android. Measured against the same rates this file already uses (one `maestro test` spawn per `ctx.run()`, a non-clearing relaunch to a ready Home), that is **≈ +95 s on iOS and ≈ +135 s on Android**.

**The ceiling stays at twelve minutes, and it is not being stretched to fit.** These budgets are tighten-only, so a suite that grows is not answered by widening the number that was supposed to notice. What pays for the trio is W1: the lane now installs a **release build with the bundle embedded**, so every member stops paying the dev-client costs the twelve minutes were derived against — a 300-second Metro bundle prewarm, a cold-bundle first launch measured at ~43 seconds, a dev-launcher `openLink` round trip and a developer-menu sheet, on every flow. Two minutes of new assertion against several minutes of deleted harness is why the number holds.

That is still arithmetic. The first three real runs on the rebuilt lane replace all of it: `bun run check:mobile-suite-budgets` reads `../ledger/durations.json` and fails this ceiling once it sits more than 1.5x above the observed p95, with the number to lower it to.
