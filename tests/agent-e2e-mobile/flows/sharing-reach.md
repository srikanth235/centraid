# sharing-reach

**Claim:** a person becomes shareable-with only by being LINKED, and the phone says so — the sheet opens, the linking surface loads, and offline the verb is withheld with its own sentence rather than offered and refused. If this passes when it should not, a release ships in which the mobile seat offers a share it cannot deliver.

**Goal:** prove that the phone can compile a share from the one subject it is allowed to share, that the sheet which compiles it opens, and that the surface where a person becomes reachable loads — with the offline half asserting that the verb is withheld and says why, rather than offered and refused.

Sharing had no mobile-owned journey. Between [#831](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/831) and the v17 rebuild ([#872](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/872)) no mobile seat could share at all, and nothing on this layer would have noticed: the mobile journeys covered reading, recording and the Locker gate, and the sharing rows in `tests/claims.json` were owned by desktop (`household.spec.ts`) and the gateway (`owners-routes.test.ts`). This flow is the mobile row.

**Setup:** install a development build, start Metro, and expose a reachable gateway through `MAESTRO_GATEWAY_URL`. The flow seeds the Tally demo scenario (`ctx.ensureDemo("tally")`) because a group is the subject being shared, then pairs with `ctx.configureGateway()`. It runs in its own `sharing` suite rather than inside `home-apps`: it restarts the app twice and, on Android, takes the device offline, neither of which belongs in a suite whose later members reuse one paired profile and one continuous session.

**Automated steps:**

1. **The producer.** Open Tally, cross to the Groups place, open the seeded group's ledger, scroll to the group's life-acts section, assert `Share group` carries its own meta (`each member you are linked with gets it in their own vault` — `apps/mobile/src/apps/tally/tally-seat-copy.ts`), press it, and assert the share sheet's general-access sentence (`Everyone you add gets the full shared item …` — `apps/mobile/src/kit/share/ShareSheet.tsx`). Close the sheet and assert the ledger is back.
2. **The linking seat.** Relaunch, open Settings from the band's More tab through the all-apps sheet, scroll to the Sharing row, and assert the screen's two halves: the ceremony (`LINK WITH SOMEONE`, and the ticket field by its accessible name `Pasted link ticket`) and the roster it writes (`PEOPLE`, by handle `sharing-people`). An approved vault link is the whole mechanism by which anybody becomes shareable-with, so those two are the screen.

   **What that replaced ([#890](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/890) W2):** this step used to open a vault drawer — `Open vault menu` → `GO TO` → `.*Settings`. None of those three strings exists anywhere in `apps/mobile/src`; the v17 shell ships no drawer, and `screens/home/AllAppsSheet.tsx` says so at the handle that replaced them. The old path failed loudly (the helper's first `tapOn` is not optional, and a `tapOn` matching nothing errors) rather than navigating nowhere, but it was still red for a reason unrelated to this flow's claim. The route is now `home-band-more` → `home-all-apps` → `home-place-settings` → `settings-sharing-row` → `sharing-screen`, each hop by handle and each waiting on the _next_ surface's handle.

3. **Android only — the withheld verb.** Return to the same group ledger, enable the OS airplane mode _while the ledger is on screen_, and assert the row swaps to `Sharing needs a gateway connection · it cannot be queued` **and that the share meta is gone**. Not both, and not a greyed control. The row itself (`tally-share-verb`) is asserted still present, because that is what separates _withheld with a reason_ from _hidden_. Restored in a `finally`.

**Selectors.** Chrome is found by handle — `tally-band-groups`, `tally-share-verb`, `shell-share-sheet`, `shell-share-sheet-cancel`, `home-band-more`, `home-all-apps`, `home-place-settings`, `settings-screen`, `settings-sharing-row`, `sharing-screen`, `sharing-people` — and content by its own words: the seeded group name, and every sentence this flow's three claims rest on. The sentences stay asserted beside the handles rather than instead of them; a handle proves a control was drawn, the sentence is what the control promises.

**What CI can prove, and what it cannot.** The nightly fixture (`tests/agent-e2e-mobile/lib/ci-gateway.mjs`) boots **one** gateway with **one** owner vault, redeemed through a single pairing ticket. That is enough to prove the producer exists, that the sheet opens, and that the linking surface loads. It is **not** enough to exercise a cross-vault grant end to end: with one vault there is nobody to be linked to, so the sheet's roster is empty by construction and the flow deliberately asserts the sheet rather than a roster it does not have. Real grant compilation, closure confinement and receipt authority are owned by `packages/vault/src/share/closure-confinement.contract.test.ts` and `packages/server/src/serve/share-receipt-authority.contract.test.ts`; the desktop's `household.spec.ts` owns the multi-seat roster journey. Widening this flow to a genuine two-vault share means a two-vault CI fixture, which is a harness change and not a flow change.

**Companion component tiers:** `apps/mobile/src/apps/tally/TallyShareGroup.test.tsx` (the verb, the subject it hands the engine, and the offline sentence, on every platform) and `apps/mobile/src/kit/share/ShareSheet.test.tsx` (the sheet itself). The device flow is the proof that the two are actually mounted on a real screen, in a real navigation stack, against a real gateway.

**Verdict:** PASS when the sheet opened over the group, the linking surface loaded, and — on Android — the verb was withheld with its own sentence while the gateway was out of reach.
