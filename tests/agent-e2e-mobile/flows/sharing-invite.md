# sharing-invite

**Goal:** prove that the phone can mint an invitation from the one subject it is allowed to share, that the sheet which mints it opens, and that the surface where an invitation is redeemed loads — with the offline half asserting that the verb is withheld and says why, rather than offered and refused.

Sharing had no mobile-owned journey. Between [#831](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/831) and the v17 rebuild ([#872](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/872)) no mobile seat could mint an invitation at all, and nothing on this layer would have noticed: the mobile journeys covered reading, recording and the Locker gate, and the sharing rows in `tests/matrix.json` were owned by desktop (`household.spec.ts`) and the gateway (`owners-routes.test.ts`). This flow is the mobile row.

**Setup:** install a development build, start Metro, and expose a reachable gateway through `MAESTRO_GATEWAY_URL`. The flow seeds the Tally demo scenario (`ctx.ensureDemo("tally")`) because a group is the subject being shared, then pairs with `ctx.configureGateway()`. It runs standalone rather than inside `run-home-apps-suite.mjs`: it restarts the app twice and, on Android, takes the device offline, neither of which belongs in a suite whose later members reuse one paired profile and one continuous session.

**Automated steps:**

1. **The producer.** Open Tally, cross to the Groups place, open the seeded group's ledger, scroll to the group's life-acts section, assert `Share group` carries its own meta (`one invitation each, redeemed in their own vault` — `apps/mobile/src/apps/tally/tally-seat-copy.ts`), press it, and assert the share sheet's consequence sentence (`Everyone who joins gets the full shared item …` — `apps/mobile/src/kit/share/ShareSheet.tsx`). Close the sheet and assert the ledger is back.
2. **The redeeming seat.** Relaunch, open Settings from the band's More tab through the all-apps sheet, scroll to the Sharing row, and assert the redemption section (`REDEEM A SHARED-SPACE INVITE`), its ordering sentence (`Create your vault first, then paste the one-time invitation here.`) and the field, by both its handle (`sharing-redeem-field`) and its accessible name (`Shared-space invitation`) — the door a `centraid://commons-invite` URI goes through.

   **What that replaced ([#890](https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid/issues/890) W2):** this step used to open a vault drawer — `Open vault menu` → `GO TO` → `.*Settings`. None of those three strings exists anywhere in `apps/mobile/src`; the v17 shell ships no drawer, and `screens/home/AllAppsSheet.tsx` says so at the handle that replaced them. The old path failed loudly (the helper's first `tapOn` is not optional, and a `tapOn` matching nothing errors) rather than navigating nowhere, but it was still red for a reason unrelated to this flow's claim. The route is now `home-band-more` → `home-all-apps` → `home-place-settings` → `settings-sharing-row` → `sharing-redeem`, each hop by handle and each waiting on the _next_ surface's handle.

3. **Android only — the withheld verb.** Return to the same group ledger, enable the OS airplane mode _while the ledger is on screen_, and assert the row swaps to `Sharing needs a gateway connection · an invitation cannot be queued` **and that the invitation meta is gone**. Not both, and not a greyed control. The row itself (`tally-share-verb`) is asserted still present, because that is what separates _withheld with a reason_ from _hidden_. Restored in a `finally`.

**Selectors.** Chrome is found by handle — `tally-band-groups`, `tally-share-verb`, `shell-share-sheet`, `shell-share-sheet-cancel`, `home-band-more`, `home-all-apps`, `home-place-settings`, `settings-screen`, `settings-sharing-row`, `sharing-redeem`, `sharing-redeem-field` — and content by its own words: the seeded group name, and every sentence this flow's three claims rest on. The sentences stay asserted beside the handles rather than instead of them; a handle proves a control was drawn, the sentence is what the control promises.

**What CI can prove, and what it cannot.** The nightly fixture (`tests/agent-e2e-mobile/lib/ci-gateway.mjs`) boots **one** gateway with **one** owner vault, redeemed through a single pairing ticket. That is enough to prove the producer exists, that the sheet opens, and that the redemption surface loads. It is **not** enough to exercise a cross-vault grant end to end: there is no second vault to redeem an invitation into, so what the sheet then _offers_ depends on who this vault is linked to and the flow deliberately asserts the sheet rather than a roster it does not have. Real grant compilation, closure confinement and receipt authority are owned by `packages/vault/src/share/closure-confinement.contract.test.ts` and `packages/server/src/serve/share-receipt-authority.contract.test.ts`; the desktop's `household.spec.ts` owns the multi-seat roster journey. Widening this flow to a genuine two-vault redemption means a two-vault CI fixture, which is a harness change and not a flow change.

**Companion component tiers:** `apps/mobile/src/apps/tally/TallyShareGroup.test.tsx` (the verb, the subject it hands the engine, and the offline sentence, on every platform) and `apps/mobile/src/kit/share/ShareSheet.test.tsx` (the sheet itself). The device flow is the proof that the two are actually mounted on a real screen, in a real navigation stack, against a real gateway.

**Verdict:** PASS when the sheet opened over the group, the redemption surface loaded, and — on Android — the verb was withheld with its own sentence while the gateway was out of reach.
