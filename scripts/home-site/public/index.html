Honest by design

Clear promises. Deliberate refusals.

Nothing hidden behind an upgrade plan. Every omission is a choice to keep your vault small, quiet and yours for the next ten years.

What Centraid promises

  • ✓ A read failure never pretends to be an empty screen, and “Backed up” appears only when your backup machine confirms it.
  • ✓ Trash gives you 30 days to change your mind; deleting forever genuinely erases the record.
  • ✓ Every record keeps earlier versions so an accidental edit can always be inspected and restored.
  • ✓ Yours to leave: open-format exports in every app, and one tap exports the entire vault.

What Centraid will not build

  • – No account, sign-up, subscription, hosted plan or company in the middle.
  • – No shared albums, public links, family spaces or split expenses.
  • – No cloud AI, face recognition, auto-generated memories or online maps.
  • – No desktop or web writer, and no third-party app store or plug-in marketplace.

Your life belongs on your phone, not someone else's cloud.

Centraid is pre-release open-source software built by one person. There is no App Store or Play Store listing yet—today you build the phone app and small backup program from source.
