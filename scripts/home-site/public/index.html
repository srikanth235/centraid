Centraid · one home for your digital life

All your stuff. One place. Yours.

Your photos live with one company, your notes with another, your passwords with a third. Centraid brings them home: one private collection on your own computer, with familiar apps on top — apps that finally work together, because everything they hold is in one place.

02 yours to keep

Your whole digital life fits in a folder.

Everything Centraid keeps for you lives in one ordinary folder on your computer. Not an account somewhere. Not a mystery. A folder.

That changes what you can do: back it up like any folder, move to a new computer by copying it, and if you ever stop using Centraid, everything is still right there. There's no export button because you never handed anything over.

→ get started

Ten minutes to your own vault.

# macOS · Linux · Windows — needs Bun ≥ 1.3 and Node ≥ 24
$ git clone https://github.com/srikanth235/centraid
$ cd centraid && bun install
$ bun run dev:desktop
# a spare machine or home server — devices pair to this
$ bun run build
$ centraid-gateway serve --data-dir ~/centraid-data
$ centraid-gateway pair --data-dir ~/centraid-data --vault Local

Centraid is in beta, and today it starts from the source code — you'll want to be comfortable pasting a few commands into a terminal. That's the whole install: there is no account to create.

First launch asks your name and creates your vault. From there, install apps, pair your phone with a QR code, and ask your first question. The start guide walks the whole first day.

Apps come and go. Your photos, your notes, your people are forever. Keep them somewhere you own.
