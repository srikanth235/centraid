Centraid · the manual

Your data. Your apps. Your devices.

Centraid is personal software with the ownership inverted. One vault on your own phone holds the canonical record of your life on a single shared ontology — no silos. Apps are thin projections over it, installed under grants you sign — worked for you by agents. Devices keep it safe: gateways you run hold sealed copies, reached directly with no middleman.

Three ways in. Pick the one you need right now.

Install from source, open the desktop shell, get a vault, pair your phone, install your first app, and let an automation pull your first external data in. No theory — a straight line from git clone to a living vault.
