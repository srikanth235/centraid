Path 1 · use it

Your phone. Your vault.

Centraid is a pre-release personal vault for iOS and Android. The phone is the only place that writes your vault. Its native shell works offline, with nine first-party apps already on Home. There is no desktop client or hosted account. This guide covers building the phone app, using its apps, and arranging a backup.

§01 · install

Build the phone app.

Source-build instructions for this pre-release are maintained in mobile/README.md. Android builds need a JDK 21 and Android SDK. iOS builds need macOS, Xcode and XcodeGen. The Rust core runs on the phone; SwiftUI draws iOS and Jetpack Compose draws Android.

For Android, build the core with mobile/scripts/android-core.sh, then run cd mobile && ./gradlew -Pcentraid.android=true :androidApp:assembleDebug. The README has the current iOS steps. These are build instructions, not a claim that a simulator build proves physical-device behavior.

§02 · first open

Your phone holds the vault.

The phone creates and holds your vault. Its Rust core is the only writer. The apps are reviewed parts of the Centraid shell; you do not install them separately or grant them access. There is no account to create.

Centraid creates a recovery key for the vault. Keep its 24 words somewhere safe away from the phone. Depending on the platform, an encrypted seed may also be kept in the operating system's secure key store.

§03 · your first apps

Open an app from Home.

All nine first-party apps are already on Home: Docs, Photos, Notes, People, Calendar, Reminders, Money, Health and Locker. Tap a tile to open it. The phone asks for operating-system permission when you use a feature that needs it, such as camera access for scanning.

Try a real journey: in Docs, tap Upload a file and choose a document. In Notes, make a new note, tap Add link, then use the Link to picker to choose that document. In Reminders, add a reminder, then use Add link to connect it to the note. These are your records; Centraid does not seed sample data into your vault.

§04 · assistant

Ask on your phone.

The assistant runs on the phone with a model pinned by the release. If the pinned model is not on the phone, Chat offers its download when you ask. Any write waits for your review and confirmation; it runs only after you tap confirm.

§05 · bring your data

Import contacts in People.

In People, open Phone contacts and choose Import contacts to bring in contacts available on this phone. To import a vCard file, open More, choose Import, then Choose files. Review the proposed matches before choosing Import {n} people or Add new only.

§06 · add a backup device

Pair a gateway directly.

A gateway is a machine you control that runs centraid-gateway. It stores sealed backup data and cannot open it. The phone connects directly over pinned HTTPS; the gateway is not another app client.

On the gateway, run centraid-gateway serve --data-dir ~/centraid-gateway. Scan its pairing QR in the phone's Backup screen, then compare the safety number on the phone with the one printed by the gateway. See Devices · pairing.

§07 · keep the gateway reachable

Run it where the phone can reach it.

Build the gateway from the repository and keep its machine running and reachable at the address in its pairing payload. See Devices · operator setup. If the gateway cannot be reached, the phone still works locally. Backup can continue in a later foreground or operating-system background window.

§08 · recovery

Keep your recovery key safe.

Your recovery key is its 24 words. They derive the keys needed to restore your vault. An encrypted seed may also be held in the operating system's secure key store, which can help on a replacement phone when available. Keep a safe offline copy of the words.

The phone says a backup is complete only when a gateway has acknowledged the records and their content hashes. It reports records acknowledged while originals still wait separately. Background work depends on windows the operating system grants; force-quitting stops it until the next launch. Learn about restore in Backups · restore.
