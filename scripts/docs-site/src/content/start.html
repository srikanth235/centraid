When recovery words appear

Centraid shows the words before Home when the phone’s secure store has not synchronized the key. When it has synchronized, setup can continue to Home without showing them then. Keep the words safe whenever they are shown.

Set up a backup

Pair a backup device you control from the Backup screen in Centraid.

Pairing and reachability

Run centraid-gateway on a computer you control. Create a fresh pairing code there, enter it on the phone, and compare the safety number on both screens. The gateway must be reachable when a backup or restore needs it.

04
24 words

Keep your recovery key

Write down the 24 words and store them somewhere safe, away from your phone.

What you need to restore

Restore needs the recovery words and access to your backup device. The gateway stores sealed backup data; it does not hold the recovery key.
