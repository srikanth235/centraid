App icon concepts · round three

Six marks, each one idea drawn in a few strokes. Shown as the icon, as the bare mark, at small size, and inverted.

1 · Keystone

An arch held up by its central stone. Centraid as the piece that holds the rest of your life together. Architectural and calm, and it reads at every size.

2 · Cradle

An open hand holding a small sun. Care and "aid" in two strokes: your things are held, not taken. The warmest of the six.

3 · Spiral

One line that winds in to a point. Everything in your life drawn to one centre. Feels like motion and growth, and it doubles as a loading animation.

4 · Doorway

An arched door with the light on inside. Your own place: private, lived in, and someone home to help. Quiet at night, inviting by day.

5 · Link

Two rings woven through each other. What makes Centraid different: a note, a person and an event linked together. Crafted, but busier at small sizes.

6 · Seed

A seed with its kernel showing. Small, private, and it grows with you. Gentle and organic, though the leaf shape sits close to eco brands.
