02 · yours to keep

Your live records stay on your phone.

The phone holds the live vault and is its only writer. A backup device keeps an encrypted, sealed copy; it cannot read your records. Keep your 24-word recovery key somewhere safe. Records restore first. Originals acknowledged by backup can be fetched from a reachable backup device without the old phone. Only originals never backed up may need reimport from your source or be unavailable. Export files from individual apps when you need copies outside Centraid.

04 · backup and recovery

A sealed copy, kept by you.

Pair a backup device with a fresh code from Backup on your phone, then compare the safety number. Your phone seals the backup before sending it; the gateway stores it without being able to read it. Keep your 24-word recovery key somewhere safe.

Where it runs

One phone vault. A backup you control.

The phone runs Centraid and owns the live data. Another device can run centraid-gateway as blind storage for the phone's sealed backup.

Your phone

Open the nine built-in apps. The vault works offline, and this phone is its only writer.
