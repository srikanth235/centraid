Each one changes a rule above. Boards follow the current rule until decided.

Words

The core owns the words; the canvas borrows them.

  • Members read backup device; code and docs say gateway. The word gateway never appears on a screen.
  • Vault is the member's data; recovery key is the 24 words.
  • Every word on a board must exist in the core's strings, or be added there in the same PR. The Code map board lists the words the canvas invented.
  • Same words on iOS and Android. Platform nouns differ only where the platform does: Apple Health on iOS, Health Connect on Android.

Gestures

Native gestures, decided once for every app.

  • Swipe a row: Details · Flag · Delete (shown on Swipe). The same actions on long-press as a context menu.
  • Drag to reorder rows and Home widgets; long-press Home to rearrange.
  • Swipe the week strip to change week; swipe the Day grid to change day.
  • Pinch the Day grid to zoom hours. Decided 8 Nov: later. Not in this PR; its own change once the core models zoom.
  • No pull-to-refresh. The vault is on the phone; there is nothing to fetch.
  • Back is the platform's: edge swipe on iOS, the system back gesture on Android.

Accessibility

Measured against the cards and pages they sit on.

  • Text 4.5:1 or more. Meta and inactive band labels are #6F6B64 (5.3:1 on cards). Done text #85807A (3.9:1) is quieter on purpose.
  • Controls 3:1 or more: check circles and placeholders are #8E897F (3.5:1).
  • White words on amber #874300 reach 7.4:1. Amber sits darker than Notes yellow so the two never look alike.
  • Text size up to xxxLarge, the shell's cap today: cards reflow to one column, rows wrap, band labels stay 11pt (LargeText board). Decided 8 Nov: every size up to the largest accessibility size (AX5) is supported. Layouts reflow; nothing truncates or overlaps.
  • Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast: glass turns solid with a border (SolidBand board). The same applies before iOS 26.
  • Every icon key has a spoken label equal to its word.

Agenda notifications

Decided 8 Nov.

  • Android asks for exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM).
  • Snooze is plus 1 hour.
  • A repeating task offers only Done.
  • Date-only tasks and all-day events never notify.
  • One pipeline serves tasks and events.

Platforms

Native primitives on each platform.

  • iOS 26: Liquid Glass for floating controls; Geist for text.
  • Android: Material 3 Expressive. Tonal surfaces instead of glass, Roboto Flex, a floating toolbar for the band (Android row).
  • Parity means the same behaviour, words and selector ids, not the same pixels.

How the canvas is kept

One source for light and dark.

  • Light boards are the source. make_dark.py makes every dark board and lays out both pages: change a light board, then rerun it.
  • Never edit a dark board by hand.
  • Until Phase 1 commits the canvas to design/mockups/, the script runs from the canvas folder and the result is republished.

One graph, one owner

Decided 9 Nov. How things are joined.

  • Links: a payment is with a person, a document is about one, a photograph depicts one (tagged by hand: no face detection, no faces album), a Locker item is about an account, a place or a person and with a person. Each is set by hand in its own screen through one person picker (Money proposes the payee's person and writes only on Confirm).
  • Only a note, an event or a touch goes to the trash with the person it is about. Nothing else joined to them does, and a photograph never.
  • Notes shows about: a note about a person, event, task or document is on the shelf with its subject. Only a note about you is a journal entry.
  • Locker links stay inside Locker: hidden from every other door, no sealed value in a label or anchor.
  • One read of the graph (edges and columns together) feeds People, Notes and the assistant.
  • Restore onto the same phone replaces the older copy and never says "Moved".

Change safely, everywhere

Decided 10 Nov. Every app keeps the same promise, in the same words.

  • Move to trash removes anything the member made. Its trash lists it with Restore and Delete forever, and every trash has Empty trash under the list.
  • Only Delete forever, Empty trash and merge cannot be undone, and each is confirmed first. Every other change says what happened in the status line, with Undo.
  • An event is cancelled or moved to trash. Cancel means it is off: it stays on the calendar, struck through. Move to trash means it was a mistake. A transaction goes to trash too; Void is retired.
  • Every record keeps its earlier values. One Earlier versions row sits at the foot of every detail and opens one list, one tap to restore. Locker's earlier passwords stay hidden until revealed.
