Decisions, words and behaviour

What the boards can't show on their own. Open items wait for the owner.

Open decisions

Each one changes a rule above. Boards follow the current rule until decided.

  • DECIDED The icon (5 Nov) is a squared circle: four quarters around an open square heart (the Brahmasthan of a Vastu home: you), on a white tile, in Centraid's four colours: teal #00AA98 (top, the shell's hue), orange #F48E04, blue #5698F8 and pink #F83F6E. One family at even brightness; brand colours, not app keys. It replaces the Wheel and the Cradle.
  • DECIDED App colours (5 Nov) are chosen from each app's purpose, its Apple convention and the states inside it. Money is navy, not green, because income is green inside Money. Amber is a darker burnt orange (#874300) so it never looks like Notes yellow. Agenda takes the logo's blue hue (#266FC4; #569EF7 in dark, the logo blue itself). Docs moves from teal to petrol (#087990) because teal belongs to Centraid. People text is #7638AD, lighter so its weight matches the other apps. Inside Health every metric wears the Health colour; heart rate is not red and exercise is not green, because red and green mean a state.
  • DECIDED One palette (5 Nov). Lists, calendars and categories use the eight app hues, lighter; no red, amber or green lists. Replaces the separate content palette and list blue.
  • DECIDED New task: title and due on top, then Notes, List and a More details row (priority, repeat, tags). Fast to add, nothing hidden. Core change.
  • DECIDED Catch-up is titled "Catch up", not "Reschedule", since its action is Complete all. Core string change.
  • DECIDED Photo details stay on the photo; the editor only crops, rotates and flips, and saves a new photo.
  • DECIDED Versions: none dimmed. The current one carries the word "Current". Core alignment for Notes and Docs.
  • DECIDED Stars use the app's colour, not amber, which only means a state.
  • DECIDED Pairing stays out of first run. Home's line "Not backed up. Pair a backup device." leads to it.
  • DECIDED Priority (7 Nov) is ! / !! / !!! everywhere, instead of Now / Next / Soon; a screen reader says Low, Medium or High priority.
  • DECIDED Show Completed (7 Nov): a list counts its completed rows at its foot, with Show and Clear. Revisits the Logbook-only ruling.
  • DECIDED Events with other guests (7 Nov) can't be dragged on the grid: the model has no organiser yet, and a drag would read as moving the meeting.
  • OPEN Notifications in Agenda wave 1.

Words

The core owns the words; the canvas borrows them.

  • Members read backup device; code and docs say gateway. The word gateway never appears on a screen.
  • Vault is the member's data; recovery key is the 24 words.
  • Every word on a board must exist in the core's strings, or be added there in the same PR. The Code map board lists the words the canvas invented.
  • Same words on iOS and Android. Platform nouns differ only where the platform does: Apple Health on iOS, Health Connect on Android.

Gestures

Native gestures, decided once for every app.

  • Swipe a row: Details · Flag · Delete (shown on Swipe). The same actions on long-press as a context menu.
  • Drag to reorder rows and Home widgets; long-press Home to rearrange.
  • Swipe the week strip to change week; swipe the Day grid to change day.
  • Pinch the Day grid to zoom hours. Proposed, not yet in the core.
  • No pull-to-refresh. The vault is on the phone; there is nothing to fetch.
  • Back is the platform's: edge swipe on iOS, the system back gesture on Android.

Accessibility

Measured against the cards and pages they sit on.

  • Text 4.5:1 or more. Meta and inactive band labels are #6F6B64 (5.3:1 on cards). Done text #85807A (3.9:1) is quieter on purpose.
  • Controls 3:1 or more: check circles and placeholders are #8E897F (3.5:1).
  • White words on amber #874300 reach 7.4:1. Amber sits darker than Notes yellow so the two never look alike.
  • Text size up to xxxLarge, the shell's cap today: cards reflow to one column, rows wrap, band labels stay 11pt (LargeText board). Whether to allow the accessibility sizes is open.
  • Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast: glass turns solid with a border (SolidBand board). The same applies before iOS 26.
  • Every icon key has a spoken label equal to its word.

Platforms

Native primitives on each platform.

  • iOS 26: Liquid Glass for floating controls; Geist for text.
  • Android: Material 3 Expressive. Tonal surfaces instead of glass, Roboto Flex, a floating toolbar for the band (Android row).
  • Parity means the same behaviour, words and selector ids, not the same pixels.

How the canvas is kept

One source for light and dark.

  • Light boards are the source. make_dark.py makes every dark board and lays out both pages: change a light board, then rerun it.
  • Never edit a dark board by hand.
  • Until Phase 1 commits the canvas to design/mockups/, the script runs from the canvas folder and the result is republished.
