App icon concepts · round two

Single marks, one or two strokes, no pictures. Each shown on paper, as the app icon in ink, and at 24px.

Seed

Two arcs holding one point. Something small and yours, kept safe and growing.

Halo

A ring with one body inside, off-centre. Your life in one orbit; quiet and modern.

Fold

A single page with its corner turned. A personal record; calm, archival, a bit literary.

Crescent

A moon cradling a small star. Quiet company that is always there; very elegant, less obviously "vault".

Dawn

Two arcs rising over a line, a point at the centre. A sunrise over your own ground: hopeful, upbeat.

Corner

A frame with one filled square in its corner. A small place of your own inside a bigger world; reads as an app at any size.

Spark

A single four-point star with soft waists. Lovely and simple, but close to the "AI sparkle" many assistants use.

Arch

One arch, a light inside the doorway. Coming home, a threshold that is yours; architectural and calm.
