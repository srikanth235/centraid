Centraid icon · four quarters

A squared circle: four quarters of a life (the circle) around a square heart (the home), kept open like the Brahmasthan of a Vastu house: you. Centraid's four colours, one family at even brightness: teal (the shell's own hue), orange, blue and pink. They are brand colours, not app keys. Chosen 5 Nov 2026; the Wheel and the Cradle are on the Archive page.

Construction

  • Circle radius 34 on a 120 grid, cut on the diagonals by 5-unit gaps in the tile colour.
  • An open square centre, its corners on the four cuts, in the tile colour: the Brahmasthan, the open heart of a home. No dot.
  • White tile with a hairline edge in light; ink tile in dark.
  • The icon colours are brighter than the in-app colours, which stay dark enough for text.
