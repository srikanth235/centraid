Centraid icon · courtyard

Four interlocking rooms in a 3×3 courtyard around an open square heart (the Brahmasthan: you), echoing Centraid's widget-grid Home. Four zero-grey luminous colours — crystal turquoise, electric violet, sunlit gold and vivid rose — chosen so they glow on light and dark without borrowing another company's primary quartet. Chosen 23 Nov 2026; Four Quarters, the Wheel and the Cradle are on the Archive page.

Construction

  • An 84×84 courtyard spanning [18..102] on a 120 grid ([icon] ios_mark_share = 0.70, matching the optical weight of a 76% disc): four 54×24 rounded rooms (rx = 8) separated by 6-unit gaps around an open 24×24 square heart.
  • Each room is a 90° clockwise rotation of the top room. Gaps and heart are genuine empty space, so the mark is see-through on any surface.
  • White tile with a hairline edge in light; ink tile in dark.
  • On iOS 26 the same shape is the layered icon AppIcon.icon: flat brand-colour rooms in two diagonal depth groups (teal and gold in front, violet and rose behind) on a cool-white ground (navy to black in dark), and the system adds the frosted glass, lit rim and layer shadows. Earlier iOS gets the flat PNGs. Android's adaptive icon inscribes the rounded outer corners cleanly inside the 66dp circular safe zone.
  • The icon colours are zero-grey luminous pigments, brighter than the in-app colours, which stay dark enough for text.
