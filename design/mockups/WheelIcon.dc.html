A vault wheel: the phone is the vault. Four spokes in four app colours (Agenda, Notes, Health, People) turn around one centre, many apps held in one place. Ink tile, linen ring and hub. Chosen 5 Nov 2026 over the Cradle (now on the Archive page).

Construction

  • Ring radius 24 and hub radius 12 on a 120 grid; every stroke 9.
  • Spokes start at the ring and end at radius 40, on the diagonals, with round caps.
  • Spokes clockwise from top left: Agenda, Notes, Health, People, in their bright values.
  • Tinted and themed icons drop colour; the silhouette carries it alone.

The bare mark

Without the tile, for the assistant's avatar and small brand moments. On paper the spokes use the apps' text colours.
