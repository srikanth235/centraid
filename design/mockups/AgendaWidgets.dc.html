WidgetKit and Android home-screen widgets display the host's last Agenda read, with its update time. Opening a widget uses the active vault. New Reminder and Shortcuts open an editable draft; Return adds it after the date preview.

Say “Open Calendar in Centraid”, “Open Reminders in Centraid” or “New Reminder in Centraid” to Siri. New Reminder asks for the title, then opens the draft with its date preview.
