Design rules

One system for every screen. If a new screen needs something not here, add it here first.

Colour has one job each

Each app's colour is chosen from what the app is for, the Apple app it follows, and the states that appear inside it. In words and badges, red, amber and green only ever mean a state.

CENTRAID
Four quarters around an open square heart, the Brahmasthan: you. Centraid's four colours (teal, orange, blue, pink) are brand colours, not app keys. Teal #1F4A45 stays the shell's colour (Welcome, Home, Chat, Backup).

LISTS, CALENDARS, CATEGORIES

One palette: the same eight hues, lighter (3:1 on the page and on dark cards, so they do not change in dark). Only dots, bars, tiles and big titles, never small text. No red, amber or green lists.

Buttons

Four kinds, nothing else. The filled colour is always the app's accent, or the list's colour inside a list.

Header keys, 44pt round. Glass for tools, filled accent for +. At most two on the right.
Get startedPrimary pill, one per screen.
Back up nowTinted pill for a second action in a card.
Copy · OpenText actions in rows, in the app's accent.
ICONS

2pt line icons. Filter is always the funnel; More is always three dots. Search lives in the band; only Home keeps it in its header.

Navigation

One way back to Home: the round key in the band. Back chevrons only on pages you drill into.

PlacePlacePlaceMore
  • Band: Home key · up to four places · search key. Liquid Glass on iOS.
  • The current place is ink and bold; the rest are grey.
  • Pushed pages hide the band and show a back key.
  • Something from another app (a person, an event, a note) always shows in that app's colour.

Status words

OVERDUE COMPROMISED NO CATEGORY PAID
  • One shape for every badge: 11pt caps on a pale tint.
  • One status line per screen for "saved", "moved to trash"; never a toast.
  • Things the app can't do are said plainly, never hidden.

Dark mode

Every screen has a dark version on the Dark mode page. They are made from the light screens by make_dark.py: change the light screen, then rerun it. Never edit a dark screen by hand.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
  • Content: photos, document pages, the app icon.
  • Switch knobs stay white; list colours stay as they are.
  • Sheets sit one level up: sheet and card-on-sheet colours.
  • Glass turns dark: a warm grey at 72%.
Dark icon: the same quarters on ink.
