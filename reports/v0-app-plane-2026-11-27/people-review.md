# People review evidence (M3)

This packet records observable People behavior for owner review. It does not request or record M3 approval, and it does not mark a progress checkbox.

## Evidence scope

The Room captures below are protobuf `Room` responses from the real Rust Core runtime after registered vault commands. Android previews use Compose/Robolectric rendering of those Room protobufs. They are not device screenshots, native UI approval, or evidence that an OS action completed. The renderer produced light and dark variants.

The [durable Room fixture manifest](room-fixtures.json) lists 20 fixtures, including seven People fixtures. Compose/Robolectric rendered all 20 fixtures in both light and dark mode (40 previews total). The light and dark PNG previews are included under this report's `snapshots/` directory.

The People Phone Says fixture now performs a first registered contacts pass, a member `entity.retitle`, and a second contacts pass with a different title. Before writing the fixture, the test requires a real `RoomSays` block with source `Phone`, label `Name`, stated value `Jordan Phone Updated`, member value `Jordan Member Title`, and a nonempty take selector. This proves the captured block came from a real difference rather than an unchanged imported value.
