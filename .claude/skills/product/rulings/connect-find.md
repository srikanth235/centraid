# Connect and find

Links, attachments and search. Every item can be linked to any other, Locker items included, through one picker (see [promises](../promises.md)).

## Search

- **One index, found the way a person types.** Every typed or labelling text column is findable. A trashed entity leaves the index, and its earlier versions with it. Fuzzy matching corrects to a whole word the vault holds, never an invented one, and says "Including results for “…”" when it did. A line from 2019 is as findable as one from this month.
- **What an item said before is found, as the item.** The words of earlier versions are indexed for every kind with an Earlier versions page (note, event, task, person, document, photo, transaction, workout, Locker item); note history has no exemption. A Locker item's earlier versions are found by their plain words only. The result is the live item, once. When the words match only an earlier version the row says "In an earlier version" and opens that item's Earlier versions; when they match what it says now, or both, the row is the ordinary one. The same line and the same page in Home's search and in every app's search (Photos' search shows such a photograph as a row under the grid, with the line). A purge removes an item's earlier words from the index in the same transaction, a trashed item's leave with it, and a value is indexed where it first appears, so an item edited a hundred times does not carry a hundred copies. A kind with no Earlier versions page (a notebook, folder, album, task list, chat thread) is found by what it is called now. A link sheet (Notes' `[[` picker) offers what things are called now.
- **Search never reads a secret.** Sealed values (secret fields, one-time-code seeds, sealed files, passkey keys) and the assistant's own words are never indexed, and ciphertext is never tokenised, in a live item or in an earlier version. A Locker item is found by its plain fields (title, names, addresses, labels and dates) whether or not Locker is locked, like any other item; opening the result goes behind the wall. A chat is found by its title and the member's words only. A health reading is not searched.
- **Money searches from the band's search key, in place, like every other app (owner, 2026-11-19).** The key opens a field at the foot and its answer replaces the tab in the same screen; there is no pushed Search page with a back key of its own. A line found backs out to Money, a term that matches nothing says `Nothing matches “term”.` with the Ask row under it, and closing the field brings the tab back (`money/search.rs`).
- **Health searches in place too (owner, 2026-11-19).** The band's key opens a field at the foot of Health's home and its answer (metrics by name, workouts by kind, date, place or note, as Health's own group, with the Ask row scoped to Health) replaces the tab; a term that matches nothing says `Nothing in Health matches “term”.` with the Ask row under it. There is no pushed Health search page (`health/home.rs`, on the shared engine in `shell/search.rs`).
- **Search is the vault's, not the phone's.** No client substring matching, no per-app engine. The ✕ clears the term and the answer; Back from a result keeps the term, the answer and the scroll, so returning finds the list as it was left. Locker's empty search offers Ask like every other app's.

## Links

- **A link to a person is set by hand, in the screen that owns the thing, with one picker.** A payment's **With** row, a photo's **People in this photo**, a document's **About** and a Locker item's **Linked to** (an account, a place or a person) all pick by name from the same sheet, and a tap on one already linked takes it off. Money alone proposes: it offers the person a payee names ("With Priya?", Confirm or Not them), writes nothing until Confirm, and a "Not them" lasts until the page is opened again. A Locker link is seen only inside Locker.
- **A link to a Locker item is an ordinary link.** The picker offers Locker items, `[[title]]` resolves to one, another item's page lists it by its title, and a person's page shows a Locker item among what is with them. A link's label and anchor carry no sealed value. Following one goes to Locker's wall.
- **Both sides see a join.** A mention, a guest, a person in a photograph and a place show on both records, apart from hand-made links. A guest is a person or an address, so an event can be linked to a person with no email. Two tags, categories, workout kinds, touch kinds, relations or places that mean the same thing merge into one on their own screen, re-pointing everything that named the other. A photograph or document captured in an item is that item's home capture, not a link.
