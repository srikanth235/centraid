# Home, the shell and the nine apps

## Apps

- **Calendar and Reminders are two apps, as on iOS (owner, 2026-11-09).** Calendar owns events (Day, Week, Month, List and Year; its band is `Calendar · Calendars · More`, and "Needs reply" is a filter across upcoming events, not a tab). Reminders owns tasks in lists: a list holds tasks and a task holds one level of subtasks, never more. Its smart lists are Today, Scheduled, All, Starred and Completed, then My Lists (`task_list`), with Inbox a list among them; its band is `Reminders · More`. Star is the one mark of importance; there is no Flagged. Each has its own Home colour (Reminders is a pine teal that no state colour uses). Calendar shows a task's title, due day, due time and state on its day, every occurrence of a repeating one, and on today the overdue ones above the day. The tick on a Calendar day is `task.complete`, the same action Reminders runs, because actions belong to kinds, not apps. Opening a row goes to Reminders. The Plus adds an event in Calendar and a reminder in Reminders, with no Event or Task switch. Overdue is the top section of Reminders' Today and the group above Calendar's today; there is no "You were away" notice. Search is split the same way: Calendar finds events, Reminders finds tasks and lists. Both tiles are always on Home, each with its own invitation when empty.
- **Every app's band ends with More (owner, 2026-11-25).** More's rows come in one order: following, import and export, Trash. A one-shot tool is never a band tab. Where an app follows a phone store, the phone's own permission state is shown there in a sentence (on, paused, off or refused, how many, when); no app has a "What <app> may read" page, because apps are not principals.
- **Recurrence has one engine.** A change of timing is for one day. Guests, reminders, links and calendar belong to the whole event. "This and the following" splits the series: the old one ends at the split day and a new event starts there, and the two halves keep no link. Cancelling is always confirmed on the phone and then executes. Overlapping busy events are allowed; the screen points an overlap out and never refuses it. An event's guest is a person or an address; a person with no email can be a guest.
- **Notes are words, not databases.** Band `Notes · Notebooks · Journal · More`. Prose autosaves, a blank title is shown as the first line of the body, a body that is not on this phone is read-only so typing never silently overwrites what the member never saw. `[[` links to anything a title can resolve to, Locker items included. A journal entry is a note with a date. Pin is Notes' mark, because it is about position, not importance. Deleting a notebook that holds notes trashes them with it, and the confirm names how many; one Restore brings them all back.
- **People is a circle of people, not a CRM.** Band `People · Lists · More`. Relatives are people. Relationships read from both sides. A person has contact points, dates (birthday, anniversary, other), a role and an org. A date can carry a reminder, "Remind me", and an imported date is silent until the member asks. A catch-up is logged in one tap. A person's page shows what is with them: one section each for events, notes, photos, payments, workouts, catch-ups and documents, a Locker item among them. Deleting a person takes only what is theirs: contact points, dates and touches. Notes, workouts, events, photos, documents, payments and Locker items stay. A list never takes its members. Importing reads vCards and shows each match with someone you already have as a proposal to merge, never writing it silently; Merge keeps what was filed on either person. Export writes everyone or one list. No avatar colour picker: a hue is derived from the id.
- **Docs holds documents the member owns.** Band `Docs · Folders · More`. A folder is a place: a document sits in exactly one folder or at the top level, a folder can sit inside a folder, and a document that belongs in two places gets a tag. Sibling folders may repeat a name. Deleting a folder trashes what it holds, and the confirm names how many. A scan or photo taken inside another item is filed with that item and is not in Docs' top level. Scan and Choose file are the two ways in. "Delete forever" and "Empty trash" really destroy. A document's history is its versions; there is no activity rail.
- **The Photos library is justified rows (owner, 2026-11-19).** Band `Library · Albums · Places · More`. Each row is packed to the full width and each photograph takes the width its own shape gives it, so a panorama is wide and a portrait narrow, at the tile-size rung's height. Only the loading placeholders stay equal squares. Star is the one mark of importance; there is no Favorites. A photograph captured inside another item is filed with it and is in no Photos view. The member can zoom the grid, and the zoom is remembered.
- **Photos names places honestly.** A place is named by a ladder (a name the member gave, an offline settlement name, a phrase relative to a named place, then "A place with no name yet") and a bare coordinate is never printed as a name. A relative phrase ("3.4 km NE of Home") never leaves the phone in a title a card or export carries. Places is a tab of Photos, not an app; a place has its own page, "At this place" (events, workouts, touches and photographs), with Rename and Merge. No face surfaces, no duplicate hunting: there is no face detection and no faces album. The one carve-out is **who is in a photograph, tagged by hand** (a `depicts` link from the photograph to a person): the member names them, nothing recognises them, and it never cascades a trash.
- **Money is a ledger the member owns, seeded from imported statements.** Band `Activity · Spending · Cash flow · More`. The Plus is keypad entry, with the last-used account chosen. Every line is editable, imported ones included: what the statement said stays under it and shows as "Statement says", and a re-import keeps the member's edit and makes no duplicate. A line the member removes goes to the trash, leaves every balance, total and search result, comes back exactly on a restore, and is never brought back by a statement imported again. A line can be split across categories; its parts sum to its amount. A hand line a statement seems to match is proposed as a match, never merged silently. An account's currency can be corrected; the figures stay as written, and the correction is refused if one would lose a fraction. A line that is half of a transfer keeps its account and its direction until the member unlinks the transfer. A date order the file cannot decide is a question, never a guess. Recurring charges are detected, not stored. Categories are one stable scheme that the member can rename and merge. Rules name payees; the bank's words are kept as evidence. A receipt captured on a line is filed with it.
- **Health follows the phone's store, one way, and the member can correct it.** Band `Summary · Workouts · Vitals · More`. A followed daily total or reading is editable: the member's figure wins, "Phone says" shows the store's, and clearing the correction brings the store's figure back without a new read. Today compares whole days to the member's own typical range, never a target. On a connected day with no steps yet the tile says "Nothing yet today", never 0. Figures are computed in Rust. A workout or reading moves to trash from its page like any record, one a store states included, and the store's next read leaves it in the trash. A day's total has no trash. Health holds daily totals and readings, never a raw sensor stream. The day form saves only the figures the member touched.
- **Home is the vault and its backup.** The header's one line is the backup line. Every launchable app is a tile. Search covers every app, Locker included. Home carries no sample notice and no "fill this out".
- **Empty states teach or import; they never decorate.** Where the phone already holds the data, offer a one-tap import at the point of use. A first vault gets a few teaching starter rows only where there is no import, and each is trashable.
- **Day one is Welcome, then the one Home, filling in (owner, 2026-11-17).** Home has one layout at every stage, the widget layout of `HomeWidgets` (Calendar, Reminders, the Photos strip, the four small tiles for Money, Health, People and Locker, then Notes and Docs); it never rearranges as the vault fills. An empty widget teaches with its own state and one quiet text action, not a filled button: "Import your calendar", "Import your reminders", "Import your camera roll", "Import contacts", "Connect Apple Health", "Import a statement", "Add a document". **The import group is per platform (#1202): Reminders import is iOS only.** A phone with no Reminders source (Android) has no such import: the Reminders tile reads "Write your first reminder", and Reminders' first-open card drops the import and the "Or" and says "Tap + to write one." (the Android variants are `AndroidFirstHome` and `AndroidEmptyReminders`). **A small tile names its app** under its figure and word ("1 person" over "People"), so a glance never reads as a bare number; a word that already is the name ("6 people" under People, Locker) is not said twice. Locker reads "Set up Locker" until it has been set up, then "Locked" or "Open". Notes shows its starter note; starter rows exist only in Notes, the one app with no import. A single welcome card ("Nothing in here yet. Start with what this phone already holds.") sits at the top on day zero and is gone after the first import. **Backup has one seat: the line under the vault name.** It is empty on day zero and day one, and about a day after the first item, if nothing is paired, it reads "Not backed up yet · Pair" (once, no bottom bar). The bottom bar carries only passing notes, such as "6 people, 12 events imported · Open People". Every app's first open is one sentence on what it is for, its one import if the phone holds the data, and "Or tap + to write one." (an app with no import drops the "Or"; Docs and Money, whose one add is a file, show that add as the button). Locker's first open is two steps: set the owner check, then add the first item. **Day one ends at the first thing the member adds or imports**; the starter note ("This note is yours") does not end it. A permission refused from a day-one widget changes only that widget: the Photos strip says "Photo access is off · Open Settings", and the app says the same with Open Settings alone; there is no second ask, no alert and no badge (Calendar, Reminders and People say the same on their tiles). A refusal is never the shell's status line, which would follow the member into other apps: Reminders', Calendar's and People's cards say it with Open Settings alone, as Photos does (`CalendarRefused`, `PeopleRefused`, `RemindersRefused`). Calendar and Reminders are two apps and keep both names. **The recovery key stays after the first backup** (`RecoveryKeyPrompt`), never in onboarding. Boards: `FirstHome`, `AndroidFirstHome`, `AndroidEmptyReminders`, `FirstHomeRefused`, `FirstImports`, `HomeBackupDayTwo`, `HomeWidgets`, `FirstOpen<App>`, `LockerFirstCheck`, `LockerFirstItem`, `PhotosRefused`, `RemindersRefused`, `CalendarRefused`, `PeopleRefused`, `BackupUnpaired`.
- **A new app is a bar the owner sets.** It needs a north star (one product whose functional depth it matches), an identity hue, a place on the ring, a row in the roster, a manifest of what it reads and invokes, and year-3 evidence. Do not add one without the owner.

## The mobile shell and design

- **Rust owns every durable fact and every derived read. Views decide nothing.** Every label, sentence, chip, enabled flag and empty-state choice is computed in the shared machine. A field a view needs and Rust does not carry is a Rust gap, never a computation in Kotlin or Swift. Both shells reach parity or the parity register says why.
- **The band, pushed pages and sheets, colour and the one feedback grammar** are in [design behaviour](../../design/behaviour.md).
