# Change safely

Trash, undo and history. The behaviour (words, rows, confirms) is in [design behaviour](../../design/behaviour.md#trash); the steps to adopt it are in [the kind registry](../../change/app-plane.md#8-the-kind-registry-and-the-promise-charter).

- **Deleting has a model.** Trash first, restorable until the launch pass empties it, then purged. A holder (notebook, folder, task list, account) takes its members with it and brings back only the ones it took, and its confirm names how many. A list and an album never take their members. A person takes only what is theirs: contact points, dates and touches; notes, workouts, events, photographs, documents, payments and Locker items stay. A photograph or document captured inside an item is filed with it: it goes to the trash with the item and comes back with it, and is never listed in Trash on its own. A purge that cannot proceed refuses with a sentence naming why.
- **Trash is one rule in every app and never promises a destruction that does not exist.** Every app moves to trash, restores, deletes forever and empties its trash, behind the outlined confirm, in the kit's words ([design behaviour](../../design/behaviour.md#trash)); the vault's shared verbs destroy for every kind, so no app offers restore only.
- **One or a hundred.** Every shared verb (trash, restore, move, tag, star, archive) takes one item or many. A bulk action is one commit and one Undo; Undo repeats the inverse over the same items, and a bulk move or categorise restores the earlier versions the commit wrote. A trailing swipe moves a row to the trash with no confirm: the status line's Undo and the trash cover it.
- **Edits are one move, not a toggle.** Complete and reopen are separate commands. Prose (a note, a document's text) autosaves when typing stops; a structured form has Cancel and Save, and one "Discard changes?" sheet when dismissed with changes. A save that fails after the member left is told on Home, never logged and lost. A save checks what it replaces and says when it was stale.
- **A new item that was never saved survives the app being killed (ruling 29, 2026-11-21, extended 2026-11-25).** Every explicit-save new-item editor holds what was typed as a draft in the phone's device store, never in the vault: it is not an item until Save, never filed, searched or backed up. The relaunch reopens the editor with it. A draft is one record per vault and kind, versioned JSON (`{"v":1,"kind":…,"fields":{…},"saved_at":…}`), never proto bytes. Save, a confirmed discard and a vault switch clear it. An edit of an existing item keeps no draft (its save names the revision it replaces). **Locker keeps none**: its unsealed values never reach disk. The draft service lives in the device store (`crates/core/src/device/draft.rs`).
- **A save that matters is told.** A create ends its sitting so a double-tap cannot create twice. A write that cannot land says so with what to do ("<title>. Try again.").
