# The owner

Product calls go to the owner. A product call is anything the [product](../product/SKILL.md) skill does not settle: a non-goal to reverse, a new promise, a new dependency or outside system, a question about what the product should be.

- Put each call as options. Give a recommendation and say what each option costs.
- Ask one call at a time, with the evidence.
- Do not decide a product call by implementing it, and do not tell a session to.
- Report in plain words: what landed, what it changes for a member, what is unverified, what device checks are owed. Link [land/report.md](../land/report.md).
- Give every estimate as a clock time in the owner's time zone plus a duration.
- When the owner decides, record it in the rulings ([product/rulings](../product/rulings/)) in the same change.
