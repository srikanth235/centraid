# Apps and mobile shell

## 9. Apps

- **Agenda is the one app for a member's days.** Tasks is a vault domain, not a launchable app. The band reads Calendar, Reminders, Invites and More. Calendar offers Day, Week, Month and List; Reminders holds smart lists and My Lists. A dated task appears on its calendar day. Catch up is the title of the overdue action whose verb is Complete all. Priorities are !, !! and !!!, and lists offer Show Completed. A new reminder starts with title and due, then Notes, List and More details. Task reminders and event alerts share one notification pipeline, reconciled on changes and vault switches. All imported time alarms are preserved. Actions are Mark as done, In 1 hour and Tomorrow at 9; snoozing changes the alert, not the task’s due date. All-day reminders use 09:00 in the member’s time zone. Permission is requested when an alert first needs it; denial offers Settings in the status line and a scheduling failure offers Retry.
- **Recurrence has one engine.** A change of timing is for one day. Guests, reminders, links and calendar belong to the whole event. "This and the following" is a row of its own. Cancelling is always confirmed on the phone and then executes.
- **Notes are words, not databases.** Autosave, a blank title is shown as the first line of the body, a body that is not on this phone is read-only so typing never silently overwrites what the member never saw. `[[` links only to what a title can resolve to. A notebook is a place reached by tapping Notebooks, never a band tab.
- **People is a circle of people, not a CRM.** Relatives are people. Relationships read from both sides. A birthday is the person's own date with its reminder always on; a gift idea is a task about the person. A person holds what is written about them alone. No avatar colour picker: a hue is derived from the id.
- **Docs holds documents the member owns.** Folders are labels, not places, and may repeat a name. "Delete forever" and "Empty trash" really destroy. A document's history is its versions; there is no activity rail. Current versions carry the word Current, and other versions remain fully legible.
- **Photos names places honestly.** A place is named by a ladder (a name the member gave, an offline settlement name, a phrase relative to a named place, then "A place with no name yet") and a bare coordinate is never printed as a name. A relative phrase ("3.4 km NE of Home") never leaves the phone in a title a card or export carries. Places is a facet of Photos, not an app. No face surfaces, no duplicate hunting. Details stay on the photo; its editor only crops, rotates and flips, saving a new photo.
- **Money is a ledger of imported statements.** Imported lines are never edited except their category and a one-way void. A date order the file cannot decide is a question, never a guess. Fuzzy duplicate and transfer detection only propose and never write. Recurring charges are detected, not stored. Categories are one stable scheme. Rules name payees; the bank's words are kept as evidence.
- **Health follows the phone's store, one way.** Today compares whole days to the member's own typical range, never a target. On a connected day with no steps yet the tile says "Nothing yet today", never 0. Figures are computed in Rust.
- **Home is the vault and its backup.** The header's one line is the backup line. Every launchable app is a tile. Search covers every app, Locker included. Home carries no sample notice and no "fill this out".
- **Empty states teach or import; they never decorate.** Where the phone already holds the data, offer a one-tap import at the point of use. Teach with the empty-state sentence; do not insert sample rows in the member's vault.
- **A new app is a bar the owner sets.** It needs a north star (one product whose functional depth it matches), an identity hue, a place on the ring, a row in the roster, a manifest of what it reads and invokes, and year-3 evidence. Do not add one without the owner.

## 10. The mobile shell and design

- **Rust owns every durable fact and every derived read. Views decide nothing.** Every label, sentence, chip, enabled flag and empty-state choice is computed in the shared machine. A field a view needs and Rust does not carry is a Rust gap, never a computation in Kotlin or Swift. Both shells reach parity or the parity register says why.
- **The band is places, not features.** A Home key, up to four places including More, and the search key; More holds secondary places and actions only. Search is the round key at the band's trailing end, not a tab or a bar under the title; opened, its field takes the band's place over the keyboard. Filters and sort are one filter menu behind a funnel button in the header (a scope such as a notebook or Money's month is the title's second line). The header has at most two trailing items: the filter menu and the app's create verb. The shell band lists Home, Chat and Backup, never an installed app.
- **Content pushes; decisions sheet.** A destination the member reads is a pushed page with a real back target; a choice is a sheet. Editors hide the band. Media is a full-bleed room of its own.
- **Trash is per app and never promises a destruction that does not exist.** An app with a destroy command offers Empty trash behind the outlined confirm; an app without one offers restore only.
- **Copy is a contract.** Calm, concrete, confident, short, sentence case ("Photo deleted", not "Your photo has been successfully deleted"). One noun per destination and the screen title wins. No exception string is ever shown; the core builds the sentence from a code. State each ontology fact once, where the member first meets it.
- **Colour has one job.** The shell uses its teal; each app uses the accent in the mockups and tokens, including actions and stars. Red, amber and green in words and badges mean state. Lists, calendars and categories share one eight-hue palette. A cross-app item uses its own app's colour.
- **One feedback grammar.** State is one status line docked above the band. No toast, red-dot notification, bounce or parallax. Smart-card counts describe the collection. A load is a skeleton, not a spinner. Pressed is the one interaction state. The one haptic is a selection tick; a success haptic fires when the write resolves, never at the tap.
- **Chrome is the platform's.** Headers and tab bars are the platform's own, glass on iOS. Floating controls (the app band, its round keys, the search field, the Photos selection bar) take each platform's native look: Liquid Glass on iOS 26 (solid before it and under Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast), a solid tonal pill on Android. Content stays paper. Parity between shells is the same behaviour, words and selector ids, not the same pixels. Shared values come from design/tokens.toml. Text follows every system size, including accessibility sizes, and cards and rows reflow.
- **Ask for permissions at the moment of need, never at launch,** and leave an actionable path to Settings after a denial. Quick add routes unambiguous input deterministically; ambiguous input shows a preview and commits nothing until confirmed.
- **Member-facing screens name no host, transport or engine state.** Members read backup device; code and technical docs say gateway. The vault lockup says where the vault stands, derived and never stored.
- **A save that matters is told.** A create ends its sitting so a double-tap cannot create twice. A write that cannot land says so with what to do ("<title>. Try again.").

The full [rooms, navigation, feedback, search, accessibility and wording contracts](screens-and-copy.md) govern every screen. Visual rules live in the [mockups](../../../../design/mockups/README.md), and values in [design/tokens.toml](../../../../design/tokens.toml).
