# Apps and mobile shell

## 9. Apps

- **Calendar and Reminders are two apps, as on iOS (owner, 2026-11-09, reversing "Agenda is one app").** Calendar owns events and invites (Day, Week, Month, List, Year; its band is Calendar, Calendars and Invites, Apple's Inbox); Reminders owns tasks and lists (the smart lists Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged and Completed, then My Lists, with Inbox a list among them; its band is Reminders and More). Each has its own crate, manifest, scopes, screens, tile and Home colour (Reminders is a pine teal that no state colour uses). Calendar may READ a reminder's title, due day, due time and state to show it on its day, as Apple's does: every occurrence of a repeating one, and on today the overdue ones above the day. A reminder on a day can be ticked complete and un-ticked there, and the rule is least privilege: the tick is REMINDERS' OWN `complete-task` or `reopen-task`, sent by the Calendar screen under Reminders' name, so Reminders owns the write and Calendar holds no task write and no complete or uncomplete permission either (its manifest says exactly that and a test pins it); opening a row hands off to Reminders. Calendar never writes a task and Reminders never touches an event. The Plus adds an event in Calendar and a reminder in Reminders, with no Event or Task switch. Catch up, filing and the quick add are Reminders'; Overdue is the top section of Reminders' Today list and the group above Calendar's today, and there is no separate "You were away" notice (Apple has none; the Catch up page stays). Search is split the same way: Calendar finds events, Reminders finds tasks and lists. Both tiles are always on Home, each with its own invitation when empty.
- **Recurrence has one engine.** A change of timing is for one day. Guests, reminders, links and calendar belong to the whole event. "This and the following" is a row of its own. Cancelling is always confirmed on the phone and then executes.
- **Notes are words, not databases.** Autosave, a blank title is shown as the first line of the body, a body that is not on this phone is read-only so typing never silently overwrites what the member never saw. `[[` links only to what a title can resolve to. A notebook is a place reached by tapping Notebooks, never a band tab.
- **People is a circle of people, not a CRM.** Relatives are people. Relationships read from both sides. A birthday is the person's own date with its reminder always on; a gift idea is a task about the person. A person holds what is written about them alone: only a note, an event or a touch that is about or with them alone goes to the trash with them; a document, a payment, a photograph or a Locker item joined to them stays. "In your vault" shows events, tasks, notes, documents, payments and photographs, and never a Locker item. "Phone contacts" in More says what People may read (on, paused, off or refused, how many, when) and can pause or resume it. Import reads vCards and shows each match with someone you already have as a proposal to merge, never writing it silently; Export writes everyone or one list. No avatar colour picker: a hue is derived from the id.
- **Docs holds documents the member owns.** Folders are labels, not places, and may repeat a name. "Delete forever" and "Empty trash" really destroy. A document's history is its versions; there is no activity rail.
- **Photos names places honestly.** A place is named by a ladder (a name the member gave, an offline settlement name, a phrase relative to a named place, then "A place with no name yet") and a bare coordinate is never printed as a name. A relative phrase ("3.4 km NE of Home") never leaves the phone in a title a card or export carries. Places is a facet of Photos, not an app. No face surfaces, no duplicate hunting: there is no face detection and no faces album. The one carve-out is **who is in a photograph, tagged by hand** (a `depicts` link from the photograph to a person): the member names them, nothing recognises them, and it never cascades a trash.
- **Money is a ledger of imported statements.** Imported lines are never edited except their category and their name; the member removes one by moving it to the trash (owner, 2026-11-10: this replaces the one-way void), and it leaves every balance, total, recurring charge and search result, comes back exactly on a restore, and is never brought back by a statement imported again. A void already in a vault stays void and counts nowhere. A date order the file cannot decide is a question, never a guess. Fuzzy duplicate and transfer detection only propose and never write. Recurring charges are detected, not stored. Categories are one stable scheme. Rules name payees; the bank's words are kept as evidence.
- **A link to a person is set by hand, in the screen that owns the thing, with one picker.** A payment's **With** row, a photo's **People in this photo**, a document's **About** and a Locker item's **Linked to** (an account, a place or a person) all pick by name from the same sheet, and a tap on one already linked takes it off. Money alone proposes: it offers the person a payee names ("With Priya?", Confirm or Not them), writes nothing until Confirm, and a "Not them" lasts until the page is opened again. A Locker link is seen only inside Locker.
- **Health follows the phone's store, one way.** Today compares whole days to the member's own typical range, never a target. On a connected day with no steps yet the tile says "Nothing yet today", never 0. Figures are computed in Rust. A workout moves to trash from its page like any record, one a store states included: the store's figures are not the member's to change, but whether the member keeps the workout is, and the store's next read leaves a trashed workout in the trash. A day's total has no trash.
- **Home is the vault and its backup.** The header's one line is the backup line. Every launchable app is a tile. Search covers every app, Locker included. Home carries no sample notice and no "fill this out".
- **Empty states teach or import; they never decorate.** Where the phone already holds the data, offer a one-tap import at the point of use. A first vault gets a few teaching starter rows only where there is no import, and each is trashable.
- **A new app is a bar the owner sets.** It needs a north star (one product whose functional depth it matches), an identity hue, a place on the ring, a row in the roster, a manifest of what it reads and invokes, and year-3 evidence. Do not add one without the owner.

## 10. The mobile shell and design

- **Rust owns every durable fact and every derived read. Views decide nothing.** Every label, sentence, chip, enabled flag and empty-state choice is computed in the shared machine. A field a view needs and Rust does not carry is a Rust gap, never a computation in Kotlin or Swift. Both shells reach parity or the parity register says why.
- **The band is places, not features.** Two to four destinations then More; More holds secondary places and actions only. Search is the round key at the band's trailing end, not a tab or a bar under the title; opened, its field takes the band's place over the keyboard. Filters and sort are one filter menu behind a funnel button in the header (a scope such as a notebook or Money's month is the title's second line). The header has at most two trailing items: the filter menu and the app's create verb. The shell band lists Home, Chat and Backup, never an installed app.
- **Content pushes; decisions sheet.** A destination the member reads is a pushed page with a real back target; a choice is a sheet. Editors hide the band. Media is a full-bleed room of its own.
- **Trash is one rule in every app and never promises a destruction that does not exist.** Every app moves to trash, restores, deletes forever and empties its trash, behind the outlined confirm, in the kit's words (design-behaviour.md § Trash); the vault's shared verbs destroy for every kind, so no app offers restore only.
- **Taking data out is one rule in every app.** Every app exports its data as a file on the kit's one export screen (More, "Export"), and a primary record can be handed, one item at a time, to the OS share sheet from its detail screen ("Share"). Both ask the owner check first (a phone with no passcode goes ahead with a warning, except Locker's export, which refuses), and an import is never checked. A Locker item cannot be shared: its contents leave only through its own export. Docs shares the stored file and Photos the originals; every other app shares the item as a small file (a note as Markdown, a person as a vCard).
- **Copy is a contract.** Calm, concrete, confident, short, sentence case ("Photo deleted", not "Your photo has been successfully deleted"). One noun per destination and the screen title wins. No exception string is ever shown; the core builds the sentence from a code. State each ontology fact once, where the member first meets it.
- **Colour has one job each.** An app's colour marks that app; red, amber and green only ever mean a state; the shell's colour is its own. Commit is the one filled element per view, destructive is the outlined state-red button. Values are in `design/tokens.toml`; the rules are in [design behaviour](design-behaviour.md#colour).
- **One feedback grammar.** State is one status line docked above the band. No toast, badge count, red dot, bounce or parallax. A load is a skeleton, not a spinner. Pressed is the one interaction state. The one haptic is a selection tick; a success haptic fires when the write resolves, never at the tap.
- **Chrome is the platform's.** Headers and tab bars are the platform's own, glass on iOS. Floating controls (the app band, its round keys, the search field, the Photos selection bar) take each platform's native look: Liquid Glass on iOS 26 (system material before it, paper under Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast), a solid tonal pill on Android. Content stays paper. Parity between shells is the same behaviour, words and selector ids, not the same pixels. One row height of 44, page margin of 18, text follows the system size within a cap, text in a fixed-height box is clamped never clipped.
- **Ask for permissions at the moment of need, never at launch,** and leave an actionable path to Settings after a denial. Quick add routes unambiguous input deterministically; ambiguous input shows a preview and commits nothing until confirmed.
- **Member-facing screens name no host, transport or engine state.** A gateway is named only where the member manages one. The vault lockup says where the vault stands, derived and never stored.
- **A save that matters is told.** A create ends its sitting so a double-tap cannot create twice. A write that cannot land says so with what to do ("<title>. Try again.").
