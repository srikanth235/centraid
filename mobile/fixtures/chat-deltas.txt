# ONE SET OF CASES FOR EVERY STORE THAT APPLIES A STREAMING ANSWER'S DELTAS (the runtime's own
# `chat::delta`, ScreenStore.swift, Screens.kt). A case: `name:`, a held view (`revision:`,
# `activity:`, `messages:` as id=text pairs joined by `;`), then `--- deltas` with one
# `base|revision|message id|activity|text` per line, then `--- expect` with the view that results.
# A delta made against another revision is dropped, and the rest of the case goes on without it.
# Cases are separated by a line of four equals signs.
name: tokens append in order
revision: 5
activity: Thinking
messages: 1=hi;2=
--- deltas
5|6|2||Hello
6|7|2||, world
--- expect
revision: 7
activity:
messages: 1=hi;2=Hello, world
====

2|3|2||Spent
--- expect
revision: 3
activity:
messages: 1=q;2=Spent
====
name: a delta made against another revision is dropped
revision: 4
activity:
messages: 1=q;2=abc

--- expect
revision: 5
activity:
messages: 1=q;2=abcdef
